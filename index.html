Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WRLD Player does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or other personal information.

2. Music and Audio Files

WRLD Player may request access to audio files stored on your Android device so you can play music, browse your library, create playlists, edit audio, record audio, and export edited files.

Your music and audio files remain on your device. WRLD Player does not upload your audio files to our servers.

3. Microphone Permission

If you use the voice or audio recording features, WRLD Player requires microphone permission to record audio. Recordings are processed and stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

4. Internet Access

WRLD Player's core music playback and audio-editing features are designed to work offline. We do not use your internet connection to collect or transmit personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

WRLD Player does not use advertising or analytics services to collect personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

WRLD Player does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If WRLD Player's privacy practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the WRLD Player developer.
